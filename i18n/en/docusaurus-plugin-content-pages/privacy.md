# Website Analytics Notice

This site uses **Google Analytics 4** to understand visitor traffic.

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Data collected | Pages visited, visit time, referral source, browser and device type, approximate location (country, city) |
| Collection method | Google Analytics script and cookies |
| Purpose | Reviewing visitor statistics and improving content |
| Not collected | Information that directly identifies individuals, such as names or contact details |

- Collected data is reviewed only as aggregated statistics and is not used for any other purpose.
- You can opt out by blocking cookies in your browser or by using the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- How Google processes data: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
