## Exam at a glance

- **Name**: AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate
- **Code**: SAA-C03
- **Questions / time**: 65 questions / 130 minutes
- **Format**: multiple choice · multiple response
- **Passing score**: 720 / 1000 (scaled)
- **Fee**: USD 150 · **Valid**: 3 years
